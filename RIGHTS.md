# Distribution boundary

[LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) licenses this repository's original VFA research prose, tables, figures, and two selected UI screenshots under CC BY 4.0 and its public verification script under MIT. The licenses apply only to material for which the author holds the relevant rights.

Third-party target programs, source and binary bytes, raw Evidence, unselected screenshots, provider transcripts, private case stores and journals, and original PoC scripts are excluded. The [selected screenshots](screenshots/) show VFA's interface and summaries without a raw Evidence excerpt or a full local filesystem path. Links and hashes in [targets.csv](targets.csv) identify upstream material; they do not grant redistribution rights or change the upstream projects' licenses.
